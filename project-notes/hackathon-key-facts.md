## Current idea state — v4 (13 Sep 2026) — supersedes v3
- Full team document: `claude/najm3000-v4-block-gradient-control.md` (16 sections: physics, BGC spec, algorithms, interfaces, Grid Code mapping, KPIs, WP1–WP7, pitch + 12 jury answers, idea file EN/AR). Interactive HTML explainer published as a Claude artifact ("NAJM-3000 Block Gradient Control"); simulation `bgc_sim.py`.
- **Direction change**: (1) weather forecasting is NOT the innovation — the operator's external forecast (satellite/NWP/AI, 3–4 h ahead) is an INPUT; never say "AI predicts clouds" or "operators are blind to the weather". Message: "We don't predict the weather. We predict its operational impact and control the plant, block by block." (2) **No battery** — flexibility comes from software + existing PPC/inverters/SCADA/weather stations + the twin + optimisation.
- **Core concept = Block Gradient Control (BGC)**: plant = 30 × 100 MW spatial blocks (10 columns W→E × 3 rows, 800 m/column); each block carries coordinates, irradiance, inverter P/Q, available power Aᵢ, post-event level, cloud ETA/ETD + confidence, set-point, headroom, firm flag, slew limit. Every 10 s BGC places the required curtailment by cloud-arrival order (descend-first, weights exp(−ETA/σ), σ≈3 min), keeps a reserve slice Δ on far-ETA blocks (**Rolling Solar Reserve** R(t,Hʳ) = headroom on blocks with ETA > Hʳ, Hʳ = 5 min), slew-limits set-points (10 %/min), migrates reactive duty to shaded inverters, and releases false alarms nearest-first. Works before contact (pre-descent), during transit (sentinel-corrected ETAs, rolling reserve) and at exit (up-gradient by curtailing recovering blocks — no forecast needed).
- **Dynamic Solar Headroom (DSH)** = SAGC Active Power Delta Regulation made dynamic (only while an event is likely) and spatial (on the blocks least likely to be shaded); absorbs partial clouds entirely (thin band: flat at 2,640 MW for 46 MWh) and enables upward frequency response (2.11.13.10).
- **Physics (verified by simulation)**: P ≤ A (no storage); headroom expires at block ETA; lead-time contract **g = D/(τ + L)**; spill on the way down E = (r − g)·τ·(D/g)/2 — **allocation-neutral** (BGC spills the same as a plant-level pre-ramp; say so). Design case D1 (D = 1,800 MW, τ = 10 min): g = 150/120/90/60 MW/min needs L = 2/5/10/20 min and spills 65/155/305/605 MWh total (5/24/74/198 MWh before contact = false-alarm exposure). Headline: 1,800 MW cliff → 900 MW at 3 %/min with ≥ 10 min notice for 305 MWh ≈ 1.2 % of a day ≈ SAR 15,000.
- **Economics**: battery block 500 MW/2,000 MWh ≈ SAR 1.09 bn (≈ SAR 110–130 m/yr annualised) vs spilled sunshine at 3 %/min ≈ 2 % of annual energy ≈ 150 GWh ≈ SAR 7–8 m/yr at SAR 50/MWh. Field anchor (Marcos et al., UPNA, 38.5 MW): 4.37 % of energy at 2 %/min, 1.38 % at 5 %/min.
- **Prior art to cite, not claim**: Marcos/UPNA plant-level forecast curtailment; DLR Eye2Sky (EPJ PV 2024: 81 % fewer violations, 13 % false-positive curtailment); NREL/CAISO/First Solar 2017 headroom services (300 MW plant, 30 MW below peak); GE patent US 8,901,411 (uniform subsystem ramp limiting). **New (to our knowledge)**: block-level ETA allocation with designed rolling reserve; the g = D/(τ+L) forecast-to-gradient contract; declarable R(t,Hʳ) mapped to Delta Regulation / 5.3.8.1(iii) / 4.46 / 4.50.8.7; pre-commissioning delivery on the twin.
- **Kill sentences**: "AI predicts clouds", "operators are blind", "grid will collapse", "we store energy as headroom", "no energy is lost", "we test LVRT", "we control loads".
- Still dropped from earlier versions: Smart Load Control (DSP/TSP jurisdiction), LVRT/droop "testing", dual-track claim, MILP battery scheduler (v3).
